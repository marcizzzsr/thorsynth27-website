Rules

  • One Codabench account per team. The team leader registers and signs the DUA.
  • No manual editing of the synthetic CTs.
  • Validation and test MRIs must not be used to train or adapt the model.
  • External data and pre-trained models: policy announced at launch; anything used must be declared in the method description.
  • Members of the organizers' groups may take part but are not eligible for awards.
  • Violations may lead to disqualification.

Data terms

By joining ThorSynth you agree to the Data Use Agreement. In short:

  • Data may be used only for this challenge and for non-commercial research as defined in the DUA.
  • Redistribution of the images is not allowed.
  • No attempt to re-identify patients.
  • The organizers may publish results, rankings and method descriptions of participating teams.

The full DUA is attached to the access request on Zenodo. Zenodo record ›

Awards

The best teams of each track are invited to present their method at the ThorSynth session of ISBI 2027 in Lausanne and to co-author the joint challenge paper. Certificates and any sponsored prizes will be announced before the test phase.

FAQ

Can I enter only one track?

Yes. Submit synthetic CTs only for that track. Each track has its own ranking.

Why does registration need manual approval?

The images are clinical data. Access is granted only after the team leader's signed Data Use Agreement is received and checked.

Does Codabench run my model?

Not in the validation phase: you upload synthetic CTs and the scoring program evaluates them. The test phase will require a Docker container with your method; details will be announced later.

Which Dixon contrasts are provided?

The final list is published with the training data release.

How many people can be in a team?

There is no fixed limit, but each person may belong to one team only.
