Motivation

Chest CT is increasingly recognised as a source of opportunistic quantitative biomarkers. Fatty involution of residual thymic tissue has been associated with all-cause and cardiovascular mortality, lung cancer incidence and treatment outcomes; skeletal-muscle and adipose-tissue attenuation support sarcopenia and body-composition assessment; trabecular bone attenuation supports osteoporosis screening.

These biomarkers depend on calibrated Hounsfield Units. MRI intensities are arbitrary units that depend on relaxation, sequence and coil sensitivity, so patients imaged with thoracic MRI cannot benefit from CT-derived measures. A reliable MRI-to-CT synthesis would close that gap without ionising radiation.

Task

Generate a synthetic chest CT (sCT) from a 3T Dixon MRI of the thorax. The reference standard is photon-counting CT (PCCT), registered to the MRI geometry and resampled to 1 mm isotropic resolution.

Region-of-interest metrics

HU statistics (mean, standard deviation and percentiles) inside six anatomical regions, the ones that carry the opportunistic biomarkers.

  • Lung
  • Ascending aorta
  • Thymus
  • Bone
  • Fat
  • Muscle

Ranking

For each track, teams are ranked on every metric; the final position is the aggregated rank across metrics. Weights and tie-breaking rules are published before the validation phase.

The evaluation code will be public on GitHub, so you can score your own validation predictions locally.
